Exponential Growth

Exponential growth occurs when a quantity increases by a fixed proportion over regular time intervals. Unlike linear growth where the same amount is added each period, exponential growth multiplies by the same factor each period, leading to dramatically faster increases over time.

Mathematical Definition

Exponentially growing quantities can be expressed as: y = a × b^t, where 'a' is the initial value, 'b' is the growth factor, and 't' is time. A common form describes doubling: if something doubles every fixed period, it grows exponentially with base 2.

Relationship to Technology

Exponential growth is the mathematical foundation of the Law of Accelerating Returns. Moore's Law describes exponential growth in transistor counts, while computing power, storage capacity, and network bandwidth all exhibit exponential trends. This pattern fundamentally shapes technological progress and distinguishes it from the linear intuitions most people have.
